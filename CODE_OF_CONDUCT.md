# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a friendly, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity. We pledge to act
and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, and respectful
community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps the community:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment of any kind, personal or political
attacks, publishing others' private information without permission, and other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions) and when an individual is representing the project in public
spaces. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove,
edit, or reject contributions that are not aligned with it.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer via a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/HLLMR/openrouter-media/security/advisories/new)
or by opening a confidential channel with the repository owner. All reports
will be reviewed and handled discreetly.
